## Retained legacy illustration sources

The previous landing page used illustrations, not photographs, per an explicit
request to keep the visual tone consistently positive and never risk a
"negative vibe" a real photo's lighting/expression/context could carry.

All three illustrations in `illustrations/` are from **unDraw**
(undraw.co), an open-source illustration library (MIT-licensed; free
for commercial use, no attribution required — https://undraw.co/license).
Each was downloaded as raw SVG directly from the unDraw site and then
hand-recoloured: unDraw's own neutral/purple accent fills were swapped
for the HHH palette (`#176B63` primary teal, `#104F49` dark teal),
while skin-tone and neutral background fills were left untouched.
Nothing here is AI-generated.

| File | unDraw illustration | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| `illustrations/conversation.svg` | "Conversation" | Hero |
| `illustrations/casual-chat.svg` | "Casual Chat" | "A little more care. A lot more confidence." |
| `illustrations/sharing-knowledge.svg` | "Sharing Knowledge" | Happy Healthy Caregiver Academy |

Chosen specifically for genuinely warm, upbeat scenes (two people
talking on a bench, a relaxed group conversation, someone sharing
knowledge) — nothing clinical, nothing depicting anyone as frail,
isolated or in distress.
